Important Ground-Level Observations

  • Unscheduled Walk-Ins (69.0%): Lack of slot booking creates Kharif/Rabi sowing queues at Belagavi RSKs.
  • Repeated Follow-Up Visits (81.0%): Farmers travel 12–25 km round-trip multiple times due to zero status alerts.
  • Delayed Turnaround (61.9%): Manual register entry delays reports past 15 days; 42.9% lost paper slips.
  • Low Interpretability (73.8%): Raw numeric N-P-K (kg/ha) and EC (dS/m) values lack visual benchmarks.

Key Stakeholder Needs Addressed in This Portal

  • Online Slot Booking & Local Map (83.3%): Reserve time slots at Belagavi labs and locate RSKs easily.
  • Real-Time Stage Tracking (85.7%): Track Booked → Sample Collected → Under Analysis → Report Ready online.
  • Color-Coded Bilingual Soil Reports (88.1%): Low / Optimal / High badges in Kannada & English with crop fertilizer tips (Sugarcane 46.2%, Maize/Jowar 25.6%, Paddy 15.4%, Vegetables/Pulses 12.8%).
  • Digital Lab Admin Dashboard (100% Staff): Eliminates duplicate manual ledger registers.
